## Terminology used in this translation

The quota in section 6.3 is called **त्रि. वि. आरक्षित विपन्न कोटा** in the body of this notice and **समावेशी विपन्न आरक्षण कोटा** in its appendix form. Both are rendered here as the **TU Reserved Inclusive / Economically Disadvantaged (विपन्न) Quota**. *विपन्न* means indigent or below the poverty line, and the eligibility rules make the economic test explicit.

Other recurring abbreviations: **इ. अ. सं. (IoE)** = Institute of Engineering; **त्रि. वि. (TU)** = Tribhuvan University; **ICTC** = Information and Communication Technology Center; **NEB** = National Examination Board; **SEE** (एसईई) = Secondary Education Examination.

## Who this notice applies to, and the submission window

This notice is issued by the IOE Entrance Examination Board for candidates who **passed the 2083 entrance examination** for BE/BArch admission in academic year 2083/084, and who now wish to be admitted under one of the various quotas at IOE's constituent campuses, per **point 6** of the *"Information and Guidelines on the Bachelor's (B.E./B.Arch.) Level Entrance Examination and Campus Admission, 2083"*.

Such applicants must submit the documents listed below at the **IOE Information and Communication Technology Center (ICTC)**.

- **Submission dates:** 2083/04/20 to 2083/04/26 (BS), *excluding public holidays* (सार्वजनिक बिदाको दिन बाहेक)
- **Submission time:** 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Venue:** IoE, Information and Communication Technology Center (ICTC)
- **Notice issued:** 2083/04/19 (BS)

## Quota 6.1 — Women's Quota: no documents due now

For the **Women's Quota (महिला कोटा, section 6.1)**, no documents are to be submitted at this time. Admission under this quota is handled **by the individual campuses themselves**, in accordance with each campus's own admission schedule (भर्ना कार्यक्रम).

## Quota 6.2 — IOE / TU Teacher and Staff Quota: documents required

Documents for the **IoE / TU Teacher-Staff Quota (शिक्षक/कर्मचारी कोटा, section 6.2)**, to be submitted at IoE ICTC between 2083/04/20 and 2083/04/26 (BS), 11:00 AM–4:00 PM.

### (a) Where the applicant is the IoE / TU teacher or staff member themselves

1. An application addressed to IoE, stating which campus the applicant is a teacher/staff member of.
2. Attested copy of the Nepali citizenship certificate.
3. Attested copy of the letter of appointment. *(The applicant must hold a permanent appointment and have completed the probation period.)*
4. A prior approval letter (पूर्व स्वीकृती पत्र) from the relevant Dean's Office or the Registrar's Office.

### (b) Where the applicant is the son or daughter of an IoE / TU teacher or staff member

1. An application addressed to IoE, stating which campus's teacher/staff member the applicant is the son or daughter of.
2. Attested copy of the applicant's Nepali citizenship certificate.
3. Attested copy of the Nepali citizenship certificate of the applicant's father/mother (the TU teacher/staff member).
4. Attested copy of the letter of appointment of the applicant's father/mother (the TU teacher/staff member). *Service condition:* for IoE, the parent must hold a permanent appointment at a campus under IOE with the probation period completed, **or** must have been serving in a body under IOE for **at least one year**; for other TU teachers/staff, the parent must hold a permanent appointment, be currently serving, and have completed the probation period.
5. The **original copy** of a recommendation letter from the campus office where the teacher/staff member serves, stating that they are currently employed there as a TU teacher/staff member.
6. If the applicant's citizenship certificate does not itself establish the parent–child relationship with the TU teacher/staff member, a relationship certificate (नाता खुल्ने प्रमाण पत्र).

## Quota 6.3 — TU Reserved Inclusive / Economically Disadvantaged Quota: documents required

Documents for the **TU Reserved Inclusive / Economically Disadvantaged (विपन्न) Quota, section 6.3**, to be submitted at IoE ICTC between 2083/04/20 and 2083/04/26 (BS), 11:00 AM–4:00 PM.

1. An application addressed to IoE stating which community/communities the applicant wishes to apply under. *(The application format is provided in the appendix to this notice — reproduced in the last section of this document.)*
2. Attested copy of the Nepali citizenship certificate.
3. Attested copy of a certificate from the relevant ethnic federation / commission / foundation / District Administration Office, or a body designated by the Government of Nepal, stating that the person belongs to the relevant caste/community. **For a person from a backward remote area (पिछडिएको दुर्गम क्षेत्र):** an attested copy of a certificate from the relevant rural municipality or municipality stating that the person is a permanent resident of that place.
4. As proof of having studied **classes 8, 9 and SEE** at a government/community school: attested copies of the **class 8, 9 and SEE marksheets** (लब्धाङ्कपत्र).
5. An attested copy of a certificate from the relevant District Education Office, or the education section of the rural/urban municipality, confirming that the school attended for **classes 8, 9 and SEE** was a government/community school.
6. As proof of having studied **classes 11 and 12** (or equivalent) at a community/government school or at a TU constituent campus: an attested copy of the **class 11 and 12 (or equivalent) transcript**. *(Classes 11 and 12 must have been passed as a regular student, on the first attempt — पहिलो प्रयासमै.)*
7. An attested copy of a certificate from the relevant District Education Office, or the education section of the rural/urban municipality, confirming that the school attended for **classes 11 and 12** (or equivalent) was a government/community school. **If the applicant studied on a scholarship** from the Government of Nepal or a government-recognised institution: a certificate from the District Education Office or the National Examination Board (NEB) stating that a scholarship was received, or an attested copy of a certificate from the ward office of the school's location stating — on that office's recommendation — that the applicant studied under an NEB scholarship.
8. The **original copy** of a recommendation letter addressed to IoE from the relevant municipality, rural municipality, or ward office, stating with evidence, grounds and reasons that the applicant is of low income and falls below the poverty line according to the standards set by the Government of Nepal.

## Quota 6.4 — Quota Sponsored by Government and Government-Owned Institutions: documents required

Documents for the **Government / Government-Owned Institution Sponsored Quota (सरकारी र सरकारी स्वामित्व भएका संस्थाहरूले प्रायोजन गर्ने कोटा, section 6.4)**, to be submitted at IoE ICTC between 2083/04/20 and 2083/04/26 (BS), 11:00 AM–4:00 PM.

### (a) Where the applicant is a serving employee

1. An application addressed to IoE stating which government office the applicant serves in and which government institution wishes to sponsor them.
2. Attested copy of the Nepali citizenship certificate.
3. The **original copy** of a departmental approval letter (विभागीय स्वीकृती पत्र) permitting the employee — serving in a government office or a government-owned institution — to undertake study for the full duration of the programme: **4 years for B.E., 5 years for B.Arch.**
4. The **original copy** of a letter from the sponsoring government institution containing its commitment to sponsor. *(The sponsoring institution must be a district-level institution of the Government of Nepal, or must have been recommended by a district-level institution.)*

### (b) Where the applicant belongs to a group listed/scheduled by the Government of Nepal

1. An application addressed to IoE stating which government institution intends to sponsor the applicant.
2. Attested copy of the Nepali citizenship certificate.
3. Attested copy of a certificate from the relevant ethnic federation / commission / foundation / District Administration Office, or a body designated by the Government of Nepal, stating that the person belongs to the relevant caste/community.
4. As proof of having studied **classes 8, 9 and SEE** at a government/community school: attested copies of the **class 8, 9 and SEE marksheets**.
5. An attested copy of a certificate from the relevant District Education Office, or the education section of the rural/urban municipality, confirming that the school attended for **classes 8, 9 and SEE** was a government/community school.
6. As proof of having studied **classes 11 and 12** (or equivalent) at a community/government school or a TU constituent campus: an attested copy of the **class 11 and 12 (or equivalent) transcript**.
7. An attested copy of a certificate from the relevant District Education Office, or the education section of the rural/urban municipality, confirming that the school attended for **classes 11 and 12** (or equivalent) was a government/community school. **If the applicant studied on a scholarship** from the Government of Nepal or a government-recognised institution: a certificate from the District Education Office or the NEB stating that a scholarship was received, or an attested copy of a certificate from the ward office of the school's location stating — on that office's recommendation — that the applicant studied under an NEB scholarship.
8. An attested copy of the **decision** stating that the applicant is being sponsored for admission to a constituent campus under IoE.
9. The **original copy** of a letter from the sponsoring government institution containing its commitment to sponsor. *(The sponsoring institution must be a district-level institution of the Government of Nepal, or must have been recommended by a district-level institution.)*

> Note on part (b): unlike part (a), this sub-list carries **no** requirement that classes 11 and 12 be passed on the first attempt, and **no** departmental study-leave approval letter. That asymmetry is present in the original notice and is not a translation artifact.

## Quota 6.5 — Foreign Quota: documents required

This section appears **in English in the original notice**; it is reproduced here verbatim rather than translated.

> **5) For Foreign Quota**
> a) Application letter to IoE as a foreign applicant.
> b) Attested copy of Passport or ID with photo provided by the embassy.

> ✅ **Bring a third document as well.** The separate *Notice for Foreign Applicants* (`QuotaForeigner2083.pdf`) lists a **third** item that this notice omits: the **Score Card of the passed IoE Entrance Exam result, if applicable**. Follow the union of the two notices — **application letter, attested passport/embassy photo ID, and Score Card** — since arriving without the Score Card risks being turned away, while bringing it unnecessarily costs nothing.

## Fallback provision where this notice is silent

A closing note (पुनश्चः) in the original states: if any of the provisions listed above has been **omitted or left out** in any way, the matter shall be governed by the *"Information and Guidelines on the Bachelor's (B.E./B.Arch.) Level Entrance Examination and Campus Admission, 2083"* of TU / IoE.

In other words, that guidelines booklet — not this notice — is the controlling document wherever this notice does not speak.
