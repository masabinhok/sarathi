## Eligibility and seat reservation for foreign applicants

> Foreign applicants are eligible to apply for the **"Foreign Student Quota"** according to the guideline for BE/B.Arch Entrance Examination 2083 (2026). There is a **reservation of 1/12th of the total seats available in the full-fee scheme of each program**.

Note the scope of the fraction: the reservation is computed against the **full-fee scheme seats of each program**, not against a campus's or the Institute's total intake. The Nepali entrance notice (`EntranceDetailNotice2083.pdf`) states the same 1/12 share and adds that foreign nationals may apply **only** to full-fee programmes.

## Who must submit documents, and the deadline

> The foreign candidates who have **succeeded in the entrance examination** conducted by Entrance Examination Board 2083 (2026) and **willing to get admitted in any constituent campus** are informed to submit following documents **within 11 August 2026, in office hours**, at IoE, **Information and Communication Technology Center (ICTC)**.

- **Who:** foreign candidates who passed the 2083 (2026) entrance examination and want admission at a constituent campus
- **Deadline:** within **11 August 2026**
- **When:** during office hours
- **Where:** IoE, Information and Communication Technology Center (ICTC)

The parallel Nepali quota notice gives the equivalent window in BS as 2083/04/20–2083/04/26, with daily hours of 11:00 AM–4:00 PM excluding public holidays. **2083/04/26 BS corresponds to 11 August 2026**, so the two notices describe the same closing date.

## Documents to be submitted

> - Application letter as a foreign applicant
> - Copy of passport or identification document from concerned embassy
> - **Score Card of passed result of IoE Entrance Exam if applicable**

> ✅ **This three-item list is the one to follow.** The *Notice For Quota Documents Submission 2083* (`Quota2083.pdf`) lists only **two** items under "For Foreign Quota" — the application letter and an **attested** copy of the passport or embassy-issued photo ID — omitting the Score Card. Where the two notices differ, **the safer union applies: bring all three documents, attested.** A student who brings the Score Card when it turns out to be unnecessary loses nothing; a student who arrives without it may be turned away.
