## Result publication notice (translated from the Nepali original)

**Date: 2083/04/19**

**Subject: Notice regarding publication of results (नतीजा प्रकाशन सम्बन्धी सूचना)**

The results of candidates who sat the entrance examination for the Bachelor's (B.E./B.Arch.) level for academic year **2083/084** — conducted by the IOE Entrance Examination Board **by computer on an information-technology basis from 2083/04/11 to 2083/04/18** — are hereby published as follows, for the information of all concerned.

Taking the published results as the basis, the constituent campuses and affiliated colleges under IOE are hereby instructed to carry out and cause to be carried out the admission-related work **in accordance with the work schedule (कार्य तालिका) to be published by the Centralized Admission Monitoring Committee**.

The work schedule to be published by the IOE Centralized Admission Monitoring Committee **can be obtained from https://admission.ioe.edu.np on 2083 Shrawan 27**.

Candidates who wish to apply under the various quotas at constituent campuses — as described in **point 6** of the *"Information and Guidelines on the Bachelor's (B.E./B.Arch.) Level Entrance Examination and Campus Admission, 2083"* for academic session 083/084 — are hereby notified to apply, **together with the documents required to qualify for that quota, from 2083/04/20 to 2083/04/26**, at the **Information and Communication Technology Center (ICTC), Pulchowk**. **Thereafter they must also apply at the campus where they wish to study.**

The entrance examination **scorecard must be downloaded from the website within one month** from the date the results were published. **After that period the entrance examination scorecard will not be available.**

**Signed:** Prof. Dr. Binod Kumar Bhattarai (प्रा.डा. बिनोद कुमार भट्टराई), **Chairman (अध्यक्ष), Entrance Examination Board — 2083**

## Two-step quota application: ICTC first, then the campus

The result notice makes the sequence explicit, and it is easy to miss: a quota applicant must do **both**, in order.

1. **Apply at IOE ICTC, Pulchowk** with the quota documents, between **2083/04/20 and 2083/04/26**.
2. **Then apply separately at the campus** where they wish to study.

Completing only the ICTC submission is not sufficient. (The document checklist for step 1 is the *Notice For Quota Documents Submission 2083*, which also gives the daily submission hours as 11:00 AM–4:00 PM, excluding public holidays.)

## Pass list at a glance

The pass list occupies pages 2–122 of the source PDF, headed on every page:

> **TRIBHUVAN UNIVERSITY / INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING / ENTRANCE EXAMINATION BOARD-2083 / PASS LIST IN ALPHABETICAL ORDER**

- **Candidates on the pass list: 7,179** — numbered 1 to 7,179 in alphabetical order by applicant name.
- **Rank range: 1 to 7,179** — every passing candidate carries a distinct merit rank.
- **By gender (derived):** Male **5,668**; Female **1,511**.
- **Published on:** 2083/04/19 (printed in the footer of the list pages).
- **Footer note in the original:** *"NOTE: Form No 2083-9867 has been Expelled."* — this form number was expelled and does not appear as a passing candidate.

**Important distinction:** the serial number (Sno) in the first column is an **alphabetical position**, not a merit position. A candidate's merit standing is the **Rank** column. Being listed as "Sno 1" means the name sorts first alphabetically, not that the candidate topped the examination.

## Columns in the pass list

Each row of the list carries seven columns:

| Column | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Sno** | Serial number in alphabetical order, 1–7,179 |
| **FormNo** | Application form number, in the format `2083-NNNN` |
| **Applicant's Name** | Candidate name, in English |
| **Gender** | Male / Female |
| **District** | Candidate's district |
| **Rank** | Merit rank in the entrance examination, 1–7,179 |
| **Remarks** | Notes; blank for ordinary passing candidates. Only two values occur — see below |

Because the list is sorted by name rather than by rank, **looking up a specific candidate requires searching by name or form number**, and reading the Rank column for their standing.

## Remarks values on the pass list

Of the 7,179 rows, **46 carry a remark** and the rest are blank. Only two values occur (derived by me from the source, not stated in the document):

| Remark | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Withheld** | 38 | Result withheld. These candidates **do appear on the pass list and hold a merit rank**, but their result is marked as withheld. |
| **Foreigner** | 8 | Foreign national, admitted under the Foreign Student Quota route. These 8 rows are the **only ones with a blank District** field, since the district column applies to Nepali candidates. |

A student whose row is marked **Withheld** should not be told simply "you passed" — the remark is on their record and they need to contact the Entrance Examination Board. The eight **Foreigner** rows are subject to the separate foreign-applicant document process (see the *Notice for Foreign Applicants*).
