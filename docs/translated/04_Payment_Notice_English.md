## Why the notice was issued: month-end payment-system delays

According to information received from **Siddhartha Bank**, the fee payment systems of that bank **and also of eSewa, Khalti, connectIPS and IME Pay** may experience **some delay** until **10:00 AM on Shrawan 1**, owing to the **Asar month-end** (असार मसान्त) load.

Because of this, all applicants who intend to fill in the entrance examination application form are **requested to complete the payment process before 10:00 PM on Asar 32**, rather than waiting for the final day.

## Revised deadlines for fee payment and form submission

For the same reason, the following extensions were arranged:

| Action | Previous deadline | **Revised deadline** |
|---|---|---|
| Entrance examination **application fee payment** | Shrawan 1, **3:00 PM** | **Shrawan 1, 6:00 PM** (साँझ ६:००) |
| Entrance examination **application form** submission | *(see note below)* | **Shrawan 1, 10:00 PM** (राती १०:००) |
| **Recommended** — complete payment early to avoid system delay | — | **Before 10:00 PM on Asar 32** |

The original explicitly states the "previously only until 3:00 PM" arrangement for the **fee payment**; it presents the 10:00 PM form-filling time as part of the same new arrangement without restating the old form deadline.

**Shrawan 1 of 2083 BS is the same day as 2083/04/01**, the date used in the main entrance examination notice.

## Relationship to the main entrance notice (deadline conflict)

The *Revised Notice on the Entrance Examination* (`EntranceDetailNotice2083.pdf`, notice 08 in this set) states:

- Examination fee must be deposited by **2083/04/01, 3:00 PM**
- Application form must be completed by **2083/04/01, 5:00 PM**

This Payment Notice, dated **2083/03/32** — i.e. the **day before** that deadline — moves both:

- Fee payment: 3:00 PM → **6:00 PM**
- Form submission: 5:00 PM → **10:00 PM**

**This notice is the later and more specific document, so its times govern.** If asked about the 2083 application deadline, the correct answer is the extended one (fee by 6:00 PM, form by 10:00 PM on Shrawan 1 / 2083/04/01), with the earlier 3:00 PM / 5:00 PM times identified as superseded.
