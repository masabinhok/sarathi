# Instructions for Online Correction of an IOE Entrance Examination Application

**Source:** IOE Entrance Examination Board — *Instructions for Online Corrections.pdf* (2 pages, mixed English and Nepali)
**Translation type:** The English portions are reproduced as written; the Nepali portions (the fee schedule) are translated. Figures are unchanged.

> ✅ **This document applies to BE/BArch — confirmed.** The sample correction form is headed **"M.Sc. Entrance Examination"** in the original, but that heading is a **stale template label**: the procedure and fees on this page are the ones that apply to **BE/BArch** applicants, consistent with the **Rs. 2000 registration fee** referenced in its closing note. Treat the "M.Sc." heading as an error in the source, not as a scope limit.

## What the correction QR code may and may not be used for

The document carries a QR code labelled **IOE ENTRANCE EXAMINATION**.

> **\*\*\*\* The above QR code can only be used for correction. Do not use the QR for other fee payment \*\*\*\***

The QR code itself is an image and its encoded payment target was **not** decoded as part of this translation — students must scan it from the original PDF.

## Steps for online correction

1. **Pay the required amount using the QR code.** Do not forget to mention your **full name and form number, or voucher number** (e.g. 20XX-XXXX) in the **remarks** section while paying online.
2. **Fill up the correction form** (the sample form is reproduced in a section below).
3. **Email the correction form and the payment confirmation to entrance@ioe.edu.np.**

## Correction fee schedule

Fees are stated in the original as "Nrs." (Nepalese Rupees).

| What is being corrected | Fee |
|---|---|
| Correcting **up to 6 text fields** — e.g. Date of Birth, Address, Guardian's details, School/College Name, Percentage/CGPA, Document Number, etc. | **Rs. 150** (flat) |
| Re-uploading an uploaded **photo or Identification Document** (citizenship, passport, or the class 12 Admit Card) in **high resolution**, because the original upload was too low-resolution to be legible | **Rs. 200 per document** |
| Correcting an **error in the student's name/surname**; an **invalid photo** (one with a background); or **re-uploading an Identification Document** where a different document was uploaded in place of the citizenship certificate, passport or class 12 Admit Card, so that a new document is needed | **Rs. 500 per document** |

Note the structural difference: the text-field fee is a single flat charge covering up to six fields, while the two document-related fees are charged **per document**.

## Note on identifying your payment

> **\*\*\*Note:** Do not forget to mention **full name and form number** (e.g. 20XX-XXXX) in the remarks section while paying online.
>
> If you have not received the Form Number, please mention the **Voucher Number** you received when paying the registration fee (**Rs. 2000**).

This matters because the correction payment is matched to your application by the remarks field; a payment made without the form or voucher number cannot be linked to your application.
