## 1. Minimum eligibility to apply

**1.1 Academic requirement.** Applicants must have studied **Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in classes 11 and 12** at TU or an institution recognised by it — at Proficiency Certificate Level, Higher Secondary Level, A Level, Engineering Diploma level, or equivalent — and must have **passed with at least 45% of the total full marks**; or, where Letter Grading applies (in the case of NEB), must have obtained a **minimum C Grade in the Final Grade of every subject**.

Students who have **appeared in** (but not yet received results for) the final examination of class 12 or equivalent, or who have appeared in the **sixth-semester** examination of an engineering diploma, may also apply and sit the entrance examination.

**1.2 Foreign nationals.** Foreign nationals may apply **only to full-fee (पूर्णशुल्कीय) programmes**. At constituent campuses under IOE, seats equal to **1/12 of the full-fee seats** of a full-fee programme are reserved for foreign nationals.

> ⚠️ **Verify:** the reserved share reads clearly as **१/१२ (1/12)** in the original. Notice 02 in this set (the foreign-applicant notice, in English) states the same 1/12 share, so the two agree — but because a misread of this fraction would materially misstate seat counts, confirm against the source PDF before quoting it to a student.

## 2. How to apply, and the application deadlines

**2.1 Online only.** The applicant must personally complete the entrance examination application form hosted at **https://entrance.ioe.edu.np** and submit it online through that same website.

**2.2 Form availability window.** The online form is available **only** from **2083/03/23 at 10:00 AM** until **2083/04/01 at 5:00 PM**. Applicants are advised to complete the form at any time within that window; after the stated time, the online form closes automatically.

**2.3** The application form may be filled in at any time on the permitted days, from any location with internet access.

**2.4 Examination fee.** The entrance examination fee is **Rs. 2,000 (rupees two thousand only)**. It must be paid at any branch of **Siddhartha Bank Ltd.** within Nepal, using the special voucher prepared by that bank for the IOE entrance examination. **Once the bank voucher has been issued, the examination fee is non-refundable.**

Payment alternatives: **Siddhartha BankSmart** may be used **free of charge**. Using **Connect IPS / eSewa / Khalti wallet / IME Pay** incurs an **additional service charge as per that service's rules**. Detailed payment procedures for Connect IPS / eSewa / Khalti wallet / IME Pay or BankSmart are available at https://entrance.ioe.edu.np. When filling the online form, the **bank voucher number or Transaction ID Number** (for Connect IPS / eSewa / Khalti wallet / IME Pay) **must** be entered.

**Two separate same-day cut-offs on 2083/04/01 — both later EXTENDED:**

| Action | Deadline in this notice | **Extended deadline (applies)** |
|---|---|---|
| Examination fee must be **deposited** | 2083/04/01, 3:00 PM | **2083/04/01, 6:00 PM** |
| Application form must be **completed** | 2083/04/01, 5:00 PM | **2083/04/01, 10:00 PM** |

> ✅ **The extension is confirmed.** The *Payment Notice* (`Notice.pdf`), issued 2083/03/32 — the day before the deadline — moved both cut-offs because of Asar month-end delays in the Siddhartha Bank / eSewa / Khalti / connectIPS / IME Pay payment systems. **Answer with the extended times (6:00 PM and 10:00 PM); the 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM times printed in this notice are superseded.**

**2.5 Information to be entered on the form.** Applicants must follow the instructions given on the form and provide:

- **(a) Applicant identity:** own name and parents' names, date of birth, permanent address, details of SLC/SEE and I.Sc./10+2/A Level/Engineering Diploma (or equivalent) results, and a **recently taken colour digital photograph** with a **plain background** showing the **full forehead**. The notice writes the size as "350×300 Pixel"; the dimensions are **300 px wide × 350 px high** — a portrait image, taller than it is wide. *Photographs of any other size will not be accepted.*
- **(b) Choice of date, venue and shift:** because the number of candidates per date, venue and shift is limited, applicants should fill the form as early as possible and secure a date, venue and shift convenient to them. **Once confirmed, the examination date, venue and shift cannot be changed.**

**2.6 Identity document upload.** When filling the online form, the applicant must state the number of an identifying document bearing their photograph and signature — for example, citizenship certificate, national identity card, driving licence, passport, or the admit card of the final year/semester examination of I.Sc./10+2/Engineering Diploma — and must also **upload a digital scan of it**.

**2.7 Acknowledgement Page.** Immediately after submitting the online examination form, the applicant must **print** the Acknowledgement Page that is generated.

**2.8 Verify your name on the roster.** Applicants who have submitted the form must check the Acknowledgement Page received under point 2.7 to confirm whether their name appears under the date, venue and shift they selected. If it does not, they must contact the Entrance Examination Board **by 2083/04/04 at 1:00 PM**.

**2.9 Admit card.** From **2083/04/08** onward, admit cards (प्रवेश-पत्र) are made available online **in phases according to each candidate's examination date**. The admit card must be **downloaded and printed in colour**. It is **mandatory** for sitting the examination.

**2.10 What to bring on exam day.** Along with the admit card, candidates must bring the **original identity document (ID)** — the one whose photo identifies them under point 2.6 and which was uploaded when filling the examination form, as recorded on the admit card.

## 3. Examination format, negative marking, and admission information

**3.1 Format.** This entrance examination is conducted **by computer on an information-technology basis** and lasts **two hours**. It contains a total of **100 objective questions** drawn from **English, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry**, following the syllabus published on the entrance examination website https://entrance.ioe.edu.np.

**3.2 Negative marking.** Marks are deducted **at the rate of 10% for each wrong answer** (प्रत्येक गलत उत्तरको १०% का दरले अङ्क घटाइने छ).

> ✅ **Confirmed: the deduction is 10% per wrong answer**, taken as 10% of the marks allotted to that question. Applied to the paper structure in the syllabus (Section A = 1 mark per question, Section B = 2 marks per question), a wrong answer costs **0.1 marks in Section A** and **0.2 marks in Section B**. Unanswered questions carry no penalty — only wrong answers do.

**3.3 Further information.** Additional information on the entrance examination and admission is available in the *"Detailed Information and Guidelines on the Entrance Examination and Campus Admission — 2083"*, published later on the IOE website https://entrance.ioe.edu.np.

**3.4 Quota applicants: prepare documents early.** Candidates who wish to be admitted at constituent campuses under any of the reserved quotas — **Women's Quota, Teacher/Staff Quota, Inclusive Disadvantaged Reservation Quota, the quota reserved for government and government-owned institutions, and the quota reserved for foreign nationals** — must have **all** the documents required by the *"Detailed Information and Guidelines on the Entrance Examination and Campus Admission — 2083"* ready **before the IOE campus/college admission schedule begins**.

**3.5 Scorecard.** The entrance examination **scorecard must be downloaded from the website within one month** of the date the results are published.

## Note on Government of Nepal scholarships

**द्रष्टव्यः (Note):** Candidates who pass the Bachelor's B.E./B.Arch. entrance examination conducted by the TU Institute of Engineering for academic year 2083/084 shall be **eligible to be candidates for the higher-education scholarships in engineering subjects** provided in 2083/084 by the **Ministry of Education, Science and Technology** — described in the original as the Ministry of Education and Sports (शिक्षा तथा खेलकुद मन्त्रालय) — of the Government of Nepal, **provided they meet the legal criteria specified in that Ministry's scholarship regulations**.
