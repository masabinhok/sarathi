# Paying the IOE Entrance Examination Fee with Khalti

**Source:** IOE Entrance Examination Board / Khalti — *Khalti.pdf* (10 pages)
**Original language: English.** This is a **screenshot-based walkthrough**, not prose. No translation was needed; the steps below are transcribed from the slides and their annotation callouts.

> ℹ️ **No `year` in the frontmatter** — this is an evergreen process document with no year on its face. Note however that it quotes a **Rs. 2,000 examination fee**, which *is* year-specific. If the base fee changes, this document's Rs. 2,020 total goes stale even though the procedure does not.
>
> ⚠️ The example screenshots are dated **Dec 31, 2020** and show placeholder values (e.g. transaction ID `s7eMk2`). Those are **illustrative, not real**. The bot must never present the sample transaction ID as a student's own.

## What the Khalti service costs, and what you get back

> IOE Entrance Fee is a service that allows applicants to pay for their **IOE Entrance Exam** using Khalti. The total amount will be **Rs. 2020 (Rs 2000 + Rs 20)**, with an additional fee of **Rs. 20** paid for online payment. Following payment, applicants will receive a **6-digit reference code** from Khalti, which they must enter in the IOE physical or online form.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Examination fee (transaction amount) | Rs. 2,000 |
| Khalti online payment charge | Rs. 20 |
| **Total charged** | **Rs. 2,020** |

The **Rs. 20 is a service charge for paying online** and is **not** part of the examination fee. This matches the entrance notice, which states that paying via Khalti wallet (or Connect IPS / eSewa / IME Pay) incurs an extra charge, whereas Siddhartha BankSmart is free.

**Official video walkthrough:** bit.ly/How-To-fill-IOE-entrance-form-Khalti

## Step 1 — Find the IOE Entrance Examination service

Click on **IOE Entrance Examination** from **Popular Services** on the Khalti homepage. The service appears in the Popular Services section in **both the app and the web**; clicking it opens the IOE Fee Form.

## Step 2 — Fill in the payment form

On the **IOE Entrance Examination Fee** form:

- **Applicant Name** — enter the applicant's full name.
- **Date Of Birth (In AD)** — enter it in the format shown, **MM-DD-YYYY**.
- **Payment** — on the **web** platform, select **'Entrance Examination Fee'** from the dropdown.
- **Remarks (Optional)** — you may add remarks (such as "IOE Payment"), or skip straight to Next.
- The form displays **Amount: Rs. 2000/-**.

Click **Next**.

> ⚠️ Note the date format: this Khalti form asks for **MM-DD-YYYY**, whereas the IOE registration form itself uses **YYYY/MM/DD**. Entering the date in the wrong order is an easy and consequential mistake, because the name and date of birth must match between the two systems.

## Step 3 — Choose a payment option and pay

The payment summary shows:

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Transaction Amount | Rs. 2000 |
| Charge | Rs. 20 |
| **Total Amount** | **Rs. 2020** |

Under **Select Payment Option**, choose either a **Linked account** (a bank account from the dropdown) **or** one of **Wallet / eBanking / Mobile Banking**. The **Linked account option is only displayed if a bank account is linked**. A coupon may also be applied ("Have a coupon?").

Click **Pay**.

## Step 4 — Confirm the payment

A **Confirm Payment** box is displayed so the user can confirm the payment amount and the service chosen. It shows **Service: IOE Entrance Examination Fee** and **Amount: Rs. 2000/-**, and asks "Do you want to continue?".

Click **Continue** (or Cancel to abort).

## Step 5 — Save the Transaction/Voucher ID

A **Payment Successful** message appears:

> Payment for IOE Entrance Examination. Your IOE Entrance transaction ID is **[6-digit code]**. Please use this ID to fill in the form.

After successful payment, a **6-digit Voucher No / Transaction ID** is provided to the applicant, which **must be used while filling the examination form**. Click **Okay**.

**Record this code before dismissing the dialog** — it is the only value linking the payment to the application.

## Step 6 — Recovering the reference code from Transaction History

If the code was not saved, it can be recovered from the Khalti app:

1. Click **Transactions** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. The overall transaction details are displayed; open the IOE Entrance Examination Fee entry.
3. In the transaction detail, find the **Id** field. **The last 6 digits of the reference code are the IOE Transaction/Voucher No.** to be used when filling the IOE form.

The transaction detail view also lists Amount, Type, State (Completed), Created On, Balance, Vendor, Product, and Other Details (IOE Transaction ID, Applicant Name, DOB, Payment, Amount, Reference, Remarks). The receipt can also be **downloaded** for future reference or printing.

Note that the transaction history shows the payment as **two separate lines** — Rs. 2,000 for the examination fee and Rs. 20 for the fee charge.

## Step 7 — Enter matching details on the IOE form

On the IOE **Entrance Examination Online Form Registration** page (Student Registration → 1. Basic Information), fill in the **same Name, Date of Birth (in AD) and the 6-digit Voucher ID as used in Khalti**. The highlighted fields on the form are:

- **Voucher No** — the 6-digit code from Khalti
- **Name** — First Name, Middle Name, Last Name
- **Date of Birth** — format **YYYY/MM/DD**, with a **B.S. / A.D.** selector (A.D. is selected in the example)

Other fields on the same form: Gender, Contact No (for a landline, the STD code must be specified, e.g. 014003000), Email, Identification Type, Identification Document No, and Your Photo.

**Photo requirement as stated on the registration form:** the picture must be in **.JPG, .PNG, .BMP or .GIF** format, with **width and height 300px by 350px**, and **size less than 200 KB**.

> ✅ **Photo size confirmed: 300 px wide × 350 px high** — a portrait image, taller than it is wide. The 2083 entrance notice writes the same requirement as "350×300 Pixel", with the dimensions in the opposite order; both describe this one portrait-shaped image. The notice adds requirements this form does not mention: **plain background, full forehead visible, recently taken, colour**.
