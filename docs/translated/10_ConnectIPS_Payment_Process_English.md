# Paying the IOE Entrance Examination Fee with connectIPS

**Source:** IOE Entrance Examination Board / connectIPS (Nepal Clearing House Limited) — *ConnectIPS.pdf* (6 pages)
**Original language: English.** This is a **screenshot-based walkthrough**, not prose. No translation was needed; the steps below are transcribed from the slide captions and the screenshots they annotate.

> ℹ️ **No `year` in the frontmatter** — evergreen process document, no year on its face. It does show a **Rs. 2,000** examination fee in the example screenshots, which is year-specific.
>
> ⚠️ Screenshot values (Transaction Id `87647`, Applicant Name `Test`, mobile `9800000000`, dated 2021-01-01) are **placeholders from a demo transaction**, not real data.

## Cost of paying through connectIPS

The example transaction in the guide shows:

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Transaction Amount | Rs. 2,000.00 |
| **Charge Amount** | **Rs. 5.00** |
| Currency | NPR |

The **Rs. 5 charge** is the connectIPS service fee, separate from the examination fee. (For comparison, the Khalti guide shows a Rs. 20 charge; the entrance notice states that Siddhartha BankSmart is free while connectIPS/eSewa/Khalti/IME Pay carry a service charge.)

> ⚠️ **Verify the Rs. 5 figure before quoting it.** It is read from a screenshot of a demo transaction dated 2021, not from a fee schedule, and payment-service charges change without the guide being reissued.

## Step 1 — Log in

Log in to **www.connectips.com** and enter your **Username** and **Password**, then click **Sign In**. (Users without an account can use the **Create User** link.)

## Step 2 — Find the IOE Entrance service

Go to the **Creditors Payment** menu in the left sidebar and select **IOE Entrance** from the options. The Creditors Payment menu also lists Travel & Tours, Airlines, Corporate, Hire Purchase, E-Commerce, and School/College — **IOE Entrance is the correct entry**.

## Step 3 — Enter the payment details

On the **IOE ENTRANCE FEE** form, select your bank account and enter all required details:

- **Account Number** — select your bank account from the dropdown
- **Transaction Amount** — 2000.00
- **Institution Name** — IOE Entrance Fee Payment
- **Mobile Number**
- **Applicant Name**
- **Date of Birth** — entered in the example as **10-24-2002**, i.e. **MM-DD-YYYY**

Click **Transfer**.

> ⚠️ As with the Khalti flow, the date of birth here is in **MM-DD-YYYY** order while the IOE registration form uses **YYYY/MM/DD**. The applicant name and date of birth should match those used on the IOE form.

## Step 4 — Confirm and authorise

On the **CONFIRM PAYMENT** screen, verify your transaction details, then enter your **Transaction Password** and click **Submit**.

The confirmation screen displays:

- **Sender details:** Bank Name, Branch Name, Account Number, Account Name
- **Beneficiary details:** Transaction Id, Transaction Date, Institution Name, Mobile Number, Applicant Name, Date of Birth, Transaction Amount, **Charge Amount**

A **Back** button returns to the previous screen without submitting.

## Step 5 — Use the Transaction/Voucher ID on the IOE form

Upon confirmation of transaction success, **log in to www.entrance.ioe.edu.np and enter the Transaction / Voucher ID to complete the application.**

The **Transaction Details** screen shows "Transaction Successful" along with:

- **Transaction Id** — this is the value to enter on the IOE form
- Transaction Date, Type (IOE Entrance Fee), Channel (WEBBP)
- Mobile Number, Applicant Name, Date of Birth
- Currency (NPR), Amount (2,000.00), Charge Amount (5.00)
- **Transaction Status:** Debit Status SUCCESS, Credit Status SUCCESS
- Sender Details (Bank Name, Branch Name, Account No, Account Name) and Beneficiary Details (Creditor Name: IOE Entrance Payment; App Name: IOE Entrance Fee Payment)

The transaction details can be **downloaded** using the Download button. **Record the Transaction Id** — paying is not sufficient on its own; the ID must be entered on the IOE application form for the payment to be matched to the application.
