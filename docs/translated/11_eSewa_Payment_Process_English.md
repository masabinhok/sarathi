# Paying the IOE Entrance Examination Fee with eSewa

**Source:** IOE Entrance Examination Board / eSewa ("Payment Service Provider licensed by Nepal Rastra Bank") — *eSewa.pdf* (6 pages)
**Original language: English.** This is a **screenshot-based walkthrough**, not prose. No translation was needed; the steps below are transcribed from the slide captions and screenshot annotations.

> ℹ️ **No `year` in the frontmatter** — evergreen process document with no year on its face.
>
> ⚠️ Screenshot values (transaction codes `0585LY1` and `058AVKT`, mobile `9800000000`, dated 2021-01-01, amounts of NPR 1.00) are **demo placeholders**. In particular the amount fields show **1.00** in some test screenshots and **2000** in others — the real amount is the examination fee, not either screenshot value.

## The most important rule: wait 30 minutes, then use the transaction code

The on-screen note in the eSewa payment form states:

> Please fill in asked details with utmost caution. **Payment made will be automatically updated on IOE's site after 30 minutes.** Please use **Transaction Code (7 characters alphanumeric code)** of payment received in SMS as **voucher number** while submitting form at IOE. 'Transaction code' can be alternatively obtained by tapping/clicking over the transaction made.

Three consequences a student needs to know:

1. **The voucher number is the 7-character alphanumeric Transaction Code**, delivered by SMS.
2. **There is a 30-minute lag** before the payment registers on the IOE site. Entering the code sooner may fail — this is expected, not an error.
3. If the SMS is lost, the transaction code can be recovered by **tapping/clicking the transaction** in eSewa's transaction history.

The confirmation SMS reads, in the example:

> IOE Entrance Exam Payment Successful! Use transaction code: **0585LY1** as voucher number after 30 minutes for form fill up at: http://bit.ly/IOEentrance2 — eSewa

## Paying via the eSewa website

1. **Step 1:** Visit the eSewa website — **https://esewa.com.np/**
2. **Step 2:** Log in with a valid username and password.
3. **Step 3:** Search **"Institute of Engineering"** directly from the search tab, **or** find *Institute of Engineering* in the **Popular Services** section.
4. **Step 3 (cont.):** Select **'Institute of Engineering'**.
5. **Step 4:** Fill up the required form details, verify them, and click **Proceed**.
6. **Step 5:** After successful payment, the payer receives a **confirmation message on the given phone number with the transaction code and the URL of the form**.

The web payment form asks for: **Student Name**, **Date of Birth (A.D.)** in **mm/dd/yyyy** format, **Mobile Number**, and **Amount (NPR)**. It also offers a **"Have Promocode?"** option and shows a **Total Paying Amount** before the **PROCEED** / **CLEAR** buttons.

> ⚠️ Note the date format: **mm/dd/yyyy** here, versus **YYYY/MM/DD** on the IOE registration form itself.

## Paying via the eSewa mobile app

1. **Step 1:** Open the eSewa App.
2. **Step 2:** Log in with a valid username and password (PIN). The login screen asks for **eSewa ID (Mobile/Email)** and **Password/MPIN**.
3. **Step 3:** Search **'Institute of Engineering'** in the search tab, **or** find it under **Popular Services**.
4. **Step 4:** Click **'Institute of Engineering'**.
5. **Step 5:** Fill up the required form details and click **'Submit'**.
6. **Step 6:** Verify the details and confirm the process.
7. **Step 6 (cont.):** After successful payment, the payer receives a **confirmation message on the given phone number with the transaction code and the URL of the form**.

The app form asks for **Student Name**, **Date of Birth (A.D.)**, **Mobile Number** and **Amount (NPR)**, with a **Have a promocode?** option and a **Proceed** button. The resulting **Transaction Details** screen shows Channel (GPRS), **Transaction Code**, Processed By, Date Of Birth, Alert Mobile No, and Student Name, marked **COMPLETE** with a "Payment Successful!" confirmation and a **Finish** button.

## Paying without a personal eSewa account, through agents

> Applicants who don't have their personal eSewa account **can pay their IOE application fee from the following agent's networks**, following the above procedures.

The document illustrates this with a map of Nepal showing agent counts by zone. **The counts printed on the map are:** 332, 1052, 1790, 2431, 2977, 3656, 3762, 4579, 4618, 5015, 5137, 7291, 10426 and 17691 — fourteen figures, one for each of the fourteen zones labelled (Mechi, Koshi, Sagarmatha, Janakpur, Bagmati, Narayani, Gandaki, Dhawalagiri, Lumbini, Rapti, Bheri, Seti, Mahakali, Karnali).

> ℹ️ **Per-zone attribution is not asserted here** — the balloons connect to map points by thin leader lines that cross zone boundaries, so only **Gandaki 5,137** and **Bagmati 17,691** are unambiguous. These counts are also from around 2021 and are certainly stale. The point a student needs is simply that **agent networks exist nationwide and can be used without a personal eSewa account**; the individual counts do not matter.
