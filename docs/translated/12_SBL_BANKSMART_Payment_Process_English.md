# Paying the IOE Entrance Examination Fee with Siddhartha Bank BankSmart (SBL BankSmart)

**Source:** IOE Entrance Examination Board / Siddhartha Bank Limited — *SBL BANKSMART.pdf* (3 pages)
**Original language: English.** This is a **screenshot-based walkthrough** consisting of five captioned steps and their app screenshots. No translation was needed.

> ℹ️ **No `year` in the frontmatter** — evergreen process document with no year on its face. The example form does show **Amount 2000.0**, which is the year-specific examination fee.
>
> ⚠️ The account number `00115068752` visible in the screenshot is a **demo value**, not a payee account. Students select their own account from the dropdown.

---

## Why BankSmart is the cheapest route

Siddhartha Bank is the bank that issues the IOE entrance examination voucher, and the 2083 entrance notice states that **Siddhartha BankSmart may be used free of charge**, while Connect IPS, eSewa, Khalti wallet and IME Pay add a service charge as per each service's rules. Consistent with that, **no service charge appears anywhere in this walkthrough** — the payment form shows only the fee amount itself.

## Step-by-step: paying through the BankSmart app

**Step 1 — Open SBL BankSmart and log in.**
The login screen asks for **Mobile Number** and **Password**, with a **Remember Mobile Number** option and a **LOG IN** button. Fingerprint login is also offered ("Tap to login with fingerprint"), along with an "Unable to Login? Tap Here!" link.

**Step 2 — Click Payment & Transfers.**
From the home screen (which shows a **Quick Links** panel with My Account, Requests, Send Money, Scan To Pay, Favorite Account, Invoice History, Complain, Activity Log and Alert Log), tap **Payments & Transfers** in the bottom navigation bar.

**Step 3 — Click Bill Payment.**
The **PAYMENTS & TRANSFERS** screen offers **Bill Payment**, **Topup Payment**, **Recharge Card** and **ConnectIPS**, plus Scheduled Payments and Smart Payments sections. Tap **Bill Payment**.

**Step 4 — Click IOE Entrance Fee.**
The **BILL PAYMENT** screen lists several billers — Load eSewa, **IOE Entrance Fee**, LOAD KHALTI, DAV Tuition Fee, DAV Hostel Fee, Khumbu Bijuli, and Credit Card Payment. Tap **IOE Entrance Fee**.

**Step 5 — Fill the required form and click Submit.**
On the **IOE ENTRANCE FEE** form, complete:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | Shown as **2000.0** (selectable via dropdown) |
| **APPLICANT NAME** | The applicant's name |
| **DATE OF BIRTH IN AD** | Format **MM-DD-YYYY** |
| **Account Number** | Select the paying account from the dropdown |
| **Use Promo Code** | Optional |
| **Save payment** | Optional checkbox |

Then tap **SUBMIT** (or **Cancel**).

> ⚠️ As with the other payment channels, the date of birth is entered here in **MM-DD-YYYY** order, while the IOE registration form uses **YYYY/MM/DD**. The applicant name and date of birth should match those used on the IOE application form.

## What this guide does not cover

**The walkthrough ends at Submit.** Unlike the Khalti, connectIPS and eSewa guides, it does **not** show the confirmation screen, and it does **not** say where to find the voucher or transaction number afterwards.

This is a genuine gap in the source document, not an omission in this translation. The 2083 entrance notice supplies the missing requirement: when filling the online form, the applicant must enter **the bank voucher number, or the Transaction ID Number** for the wallet-based services. A student paying by BankSmart should therefore retain the transaction reference from the app's confirmation or transaction history — but **this document does not state where that value appears**, so do not let the bot invent a location for it.
