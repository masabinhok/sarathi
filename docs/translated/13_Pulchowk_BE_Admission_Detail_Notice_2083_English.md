## Scope: how this notice relates to the IOE entrance notices

This is a **campus-level** notice from Pulchowk Campus, not from the IOE Entrance Examination Board. The division of responsibility is:

- The **IOE Entrance Examination Board** conducts the entrance exam, publishes the result and merit ranks, and receives quota documents at the ICTC, Pulchowk (2083/04/20–26).
- **Pulchowk Campus** runs its own admission process for candidates who passed that exam and want to study *at Pulchowk* — the online application, priority ordering, merit lists, and fee collection described below.

The IOE result notice stated that the Centralized Admission Monitoring Committee's work schedule would be available on **2083 Shrawan 27**; this notice is dated exactly that day and is that schedule for Pulchowk Campus.

It applies to candidates who **passed the entrance examination held in the month of Shrawan 2083** for BE/BArch admission in academic year 2083/084.

## 1. Filling the online form

The online Application Form for admission to the various bachelor's-level programmes at this campus can be filled from **2083 Shrawan 29, 10:00 AM until 2083 Bhadau 2, 3:00 PM**. The form is available on the campus admission website: **pcampus.edu.np**. After filling the form, the **Confirmation Page must be printed and kept** as proof of submission.

- When filling the online form, the applicant **must specify a priority order** according to the subjects they want.
- The applicant **must compulsorily upload a scanned copy of their citizenship certificate**. **Foreign nationals** must upload an attested copy of the **passport of the concerned country, or a certificate bearing a photograph attested by the embassy**.
- Applicants who want to apply **under a quota must compulsorily fill the online form from the quota side**.
- **Quota applicants who also want to apply for open seats must fill separate forms on both sides.** If such an applicant's name is published on the **quota** side, **their name is automatically removed from the open-side list**.

## 2. Application fee and how to pay it

**(a) Fee per subject.** Filling the online Application Form for admission costs **Rs. 500 per subject**. If an applicant applies for admission to **both** the **Regular** and the **Full Fee** programme of any one subject, **that counts as two subjects**. The fee must be **deposited in advance according to the priorities the applicant intends to list**. The fee calculation can also be done from the campus admission website.

Payment methods:

- **Bank deposit:** deposit into this campus's account at **Siddhartha Bank Limited, Patan Dhoka branch**, and obtain the voucher. In the **"Deposited by"** field, write the **applicant's full name** and the **IOE Entrance Exam Roll No. without the year prefix**. For example, if the IOE Entrance Exam Roll No. is **2083-16026**, write only **16026**.
- **eWallet:** the fee may also be paid through various eWallet service providers — **eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, and Siddhartha Bank Limited's Bank Smart**. When paying this way, enter the **applicant's full name**, the **IOE Entrance Exam Roll No. without the year** (again, `2083-16026` → `16026`), and the required amount.

**(b) Architecture surcharge.** Applicants who want to apply for the **Architecture programme** must pay an **additional Rs. 500** on top of the above, for the **Aptitude Test**.

## 4. Applying under a reserved quota

Applicants who want to apply under a reserved quota **must compulsorily fill the online form from the quota side**.

- Under **sub-clause 6.1** of the Admission Information Booklet 2083, an applicant who is **female is automatically eligible** for the Women's Quota.
- For the quotas in **sub-clauses 6.2, 6.3 and 6.4**, **only** those applicants whose names appear in the quota list published by the **IOE**, and who meet the qualifications, may apply — and only under the relevant quota for which they qualify.
- If an applicant **on the full-fee side** applies under this reservation quota, **the application made under the reservation quota is automatically cancelled**.
- If a person who obtains reservation is **proved to have submitted false particulars** to obtain the reservation facility, **their admission will be cancelled and action taken as per law**.
- Students admitted under the **sub-clause 6.3 Inclusive Disadvantaged Reservation Quota** pay **the same total fees as a Regular student**.
- For **sub-clauses 6.4(a) and 6.4(b)**, these seats are filled **on the basis of competition among the applicants sponsored by the sponsoring institution**.
- If a sponsored applicant is selected for admission, the **sponsoring body must pay the amount as a single lump sum by cheque** at the time of admission.
- **Foreign applicants** under **sub-clause 6.5** must upload, with their application, the **passport of the concerned country or a certificate bearing a photograph attested by the embassy**.

## 5. Priority-order rules — the most consequential section

**(a) There are 16 priorities.** Counting all programmes at this campus in both **Regular** and **Full Fee** variants, there are **16 priorities**. Admission lists are published according to the priority order stated on the online Application Form, on the basis of the **rank (योग्यताक्रम)** the applicant obtained in the entrance examination. Applicants must therefore rank the programmes they want, in order, among **up to 16 priorities**.

**(b) Automatic promotion upward.** If an applicant has already been admitted under a **lower** priority and their rank later reaches a **higher** priority, **their name is automatically published in the higher-priority order**.

**(c) No moving downward.** An applicant whose name has been published in a **higher** priority and who has been admitted **cannot change to a lower-priority programme**.

**(d) You must actually take the lower-priority seat to stay in the running.** Only if an applicant **gets admitted** after their name is published in a lower priority they listed will their name be published in higher-priority programmes in subsequent lists, if rank permits.

> **This is the trap in the process.** If an applicant's name is published in a lower-priority programme they specified, they **must be admitted in that lower-priority programme** in order to remain eligible to move up in later lists.
>
> **The notice's own worked example:** an applicant applies with 1st priority **Computer Engineering (Regular)**, 2nd priority **Civil (Full Fee)**, 3rd priority **Architecture (Regular)**. If that applicant's name is published in the admission list for **Architecture (Regular)** and they **do not get admitted** in Architecture, **they are excluded from the admission process entirely** — they do not remain in line for Computer or Civil.

**(e) Forced upgrade to a Full Fee programme.** If an applicant admitted in a **Regular** programme subsequently comes out in a **Full Fee** programme because they gave it a higher priority, they **must compulsorily be admitted** by depositing the additional amount required for the full-fee programme. **If they do not take admission, their existing admission is cancelled.**

> **Therefore:** applicants are explicitly advised **not to include in their priority list any programme they do not actually want**. Listing an unwanted full-fee programme above a wanted regular one can force a much larger payment or cost the seat altogether.

**(f) Opting out of further upgrades.** An applicant whose name has been published and who is being admitted may remain in the programme they were admitted to **only if they themselves submit a written application** to the Admission Committee stating that they **will not move up to a higher priority hereafter**.

## 6. Rules at the time of admission

**(a)** Once a name is published, the applicant **must be admitted by submitting the required fees and documents within the specified date and time**.

**(b)** Applicants whose names were published in an **earlier** list but who could not be admitted within the deadline, and who now wish to be admitted, **must submit an application to the Admission Committee**. Such applicants' names will be included **according to rank, and only if a seat remains vacant** in the list that comes out after the list about to be published.

## 7. Documents required at admission

- **(a)** **Score Card** showing a pass in the BE/BArch entrance examination conducted by the IOE Entrance Examination Board — **1 copy**.
- **(b)** **SEE** or equivalent **marksheet**, the **certificate** issued by the relevant board (or a **Provisional Certificate** if the certificate is not yet available), and the **character certificate** — **original and attested copy, 1 each**.
- **(c)** **Transcript** for Proficiency Certificate Level / Higher Secondary (+2) / A Level from TU or a recognised teaching institution, with **Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in classes 11 and 12** — or an engineering diploma or equivalent — passed with **at least 45% of the total full marks**, or, in the case of **NEB Letter Grading**, a **minimum C Grade in the Final Grade of every subject**; plus the **certificate** issued by the relevant board (or **Provisional Certificate**) and the **character certificate** — **original and attested copy, 1 each**.
- **(d)** **Nepali citizenship certificate** — original and attested copy, **2 copies**. For **foreign applicants**: the **passport of the concerned country or a certificate bearing a photograph attested by the embassy** — original and attested copy, **2 copies**.
- **(e)** **Recently taken passport-size photographs — 2 copies**.
- **(f)** Those who passed Proficiency Certificate Level from a **university other than TU**, or an equivalent examination from a board **other than the National Examination Board / Higher Secondary Education Council**, must submit the relevant board's **Migration Certificate** and **Equivalence Certificate** — original and attested copy, **1 each**.
- **(g)** The fees set out in the fee tables below.

## Notes and conditions on the fees

- **Examination fee revisions:** if the examination fee is revised by **TU** (for Regular students) or by **IOE** (for Full Fee students) respectively, the fee will follow that revision.
- **TU registration fee** applies **only to those not previously registered at TU**, at these rates:
  - Students who passed their examination **from within the country**: **Rs. 500**
  - Students who passed their examination **from abroad**: **Rs. 1,000**
- **Hostel maintenance fund fee** applies **only to students admitted to the hostel**.
- Apart from the above, **any other fee set by TU may change**.
- **Sponsored quota tuition rule:** per the provision of **point 6.4** of the Admission Information Booklet 2083, the tuition fee for the sponsored quota is **30 percent higher** than the tuition fee of the corresponding individual full-fee programme. *(This is consistent with the table: 46,854 × 1.30 = 60,910.)*
- **Engineering Council** registration and related fees **must be borne by the student themselves**.
- Applicants who passed the entrance examination and wish to be admitted at this campus are urged to **read the IOE Admission Information Booklet 2083 and the detailed admission notice carefully**, and to complete the specified process on the specified dates.

## 8. Admission to the B.Arch (Architecture) programme

- Applicants applying for admission to the **B.Arch (Architecture)** programme **must sit the Aptitude Test** conducted by this campus on **2083 Bhadra 04 at 8:00 AM**.
- Students who are **absent from** or **fail** the Aptitude Test are **not eligible for admission to the Architecture programme**. *(An Aptitude Test result obtained at **any** constituent campus is valid.)*
- Applicants who list Architecture among their priorities must pay an **additional Rs. 500** for the Aptitude Test.
